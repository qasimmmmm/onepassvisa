Travel document types explained

How eVisas, travel authorizations, and digital arrival cards differ — and when you need each one.

Our base assistance fee is US$59.99 per traveler for every document type. Government fees, applicable taxes, third-party charges, and optional priority review are separate.

eVisas

What it is for

A visa issued electronically before travel. Requirements depend on your passport and destination.

Prepare a passport bio-page scan and portrait photo.

What you receive

An electronic visa or confirmation when the issuing authority approves your application.

Examples: Vietnam (US$84.99) · India (US$84.99) · Thailand (US$99.99)

Travel authorizations

What it is for

Pre-travel clearance for eligible visa-exempt travelers, including ETAs and ESTA.

What you receive

An authorization linked to your passport. Admission is decided by the destination’s border authority.

Examples: UK ETA (US$85.99) · US ESTA (US$100.61) · Canada eTA (US$64.99)
Explore destinations →

Arrival cards

What it is for

A declaration of your arrival and trip details for Thailand, Singapore, or Malaysia.

Official filing is free (US$0) within 3 days before arrival. Optional assistance lets you provide your trip details after booking.

What you receive

A confirmation of your arrival declaration. An arrival card is not a visa or permission to enter.

Routes: Thailand TDAC (US$59.99) · Singapore SGAC (US$59.99) · Malaysia MDAC (US$59.99)
