eVisas, ETAs & Digital Arrival Cards Explained

OnePassVisa is a private assistance service focused exclusively on electronic travel authorizations and arrival cards. Understand which document your trip requires and how our specialist review works.

Category 1 • Electronic Visas

Tourist & Business eVisas

Official electronic visas issued prior to travel for countries such as Vietnam, India, Thailand, Saudi Arabia, UAE, Türkiye, Egypt, Cambodia, and Morocco. Requires a passport bio-page scan and a compliant portrait photo.

Popular Routes: Vietnam ($59 total) • India ($64 total) • Thailand ($79 total)

Category 2 • Visa-Waiver Pre-Screening

ETAs, ESTAs & eVisitors

Mandatory digital security clearances linked electronically to your passport chip for visa-exempt nationals visiting the United Kingdom, United States, Canada, Australia, or Kenya.

Popular Routes: UK ETA ($55 total) • US ESTA ($59 total) • Canada eTA ($39 total)
Browse ETA Destinations
Category 3 • Mandatory Digital Entry Forms

Digital Arrival Cards (TDAC, SGAC, MDAC)

Mandatory digital arrival declarations required by Thailand, Singapore, and Malaysia. Official government portals charge $0 (free) when filed within 72 hours of arrival. Our optional $29 service allows you to complete your form anytime after booking your flight, with automated window submission and barcode delivery.

Routes: Thailand TDAC ($29 total) • Singapore SGAC ($29 total) • Malaysia MDAC ($29 total)
Learn About Arrival Cards
Category 4 • In-Person Consular Visas

When an Embassy Visa Is Required

If your passport nationality requires a physical visa sticker stamped inside your passport booklet or an in-person biometric appointment at a consulate, it cannot be processed online. Our Requirements Checker will tell you plainly and link you directly to the official government portal without offering an online checkout.
