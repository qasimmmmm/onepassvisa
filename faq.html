Frequently Asked Questions

Search common questions regarding online visas, travel authorizations, passport validity, and fee refunds.

  • eVisa (Electronic Visa): An official visa issued digitally (e.g., UAE, India, Saudi Arabia, Vietnam, Japan). It carries the same legal standing as a traditional passport visa without requiring an embassy visit.
  • ETA / ESTA (Electronic Travel Authorization): A mandatory pre-travel digital clearance for visa-exempt nationals (e.g., US ESTA, UK ETA, Canada eTA, Australia ETA, Kenya eTA) linked directly to your passport number.
  • Digital Arrival Card: A mandatory pre-arrival immigration and customs declaration required prior to boarding (e.g., Singapore SGAC, Thailand TDAC, Indonesia e-CD).
Most countries (including UAE, Saudi Arabia, India, Singapore, Indonesia, Vietnam, and Egypt) require your passport to be valid for at least 6 months beyond your planned date of arrival and to have at least 2 blank pages.
