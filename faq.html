  • eVisa (Electronic Visa): A visa issued digitally (such as UAE, India, Saudi Arabia, Vietnam, or Japan) with no physical embassy visit required.
  • ETA / ESTA (Electronic Travel Authorization): A mandatory pre-travel registration for visa-exempt travelers (such as US ESTA, UK ETA, Canada eTA, Australia ETA, or Kenya eTA).
  • Digital Arrival Card: A mandatory pre-arrival customs and entry declaration required before boarding (such as Singapore SGAC, Thailand TDAC, or Bali/Indonesia).
Most destinations require your passport to be valid for at least 6 months beyond your planned arrival date and to have at least 2 blank pages.
Once approved by the destination authority, we email your travel document as a PDF file that you can save on your phone and print for airport check-in.
