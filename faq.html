Search common questions regarding passport validity, eVisa processing speeds, biometric photo rules, and fee refunds.

  • eVisa (Electronic Visa): An official visa issued digitally (e.g., UAE, India, Saudi Arabia, Vietnam, Japan). It carries the exact same legal authority as a traditional passport sticker without requiring a physical embassy visit.
  • ETA / eTA (Electronic Travel Authorization): A mandatory pre-screening clearance for visa-exempt nationals (e.g., US ESTA, UK ETA, Canada eTA, Australia ETA, Kenya eTA) linked electronically to your passport number.
  • Digital Arrival Card: A mandatory pre-arrival customs & immigration declaration required by all incoming passengers regardless of visa status (e.g., Singapore SGAC, Malaysia MDAC, Thailand TDAC, Indonesia e-CD).
The international standard across 95% of destinations (including UAE, Saudi Arabia, India, Singapore, Indonesia, Vietnam, and Egypt) requires your passport to be valid for at least 6 months beyond your planned date of arrival and to have at least 2 blank pages. For Schengen countries, your passport must be valid for at least 3 months beyond your planned departure from the Schengen zone and issued within the last 10 years.
