Official digital arrival cards for Thailand, Singapore, and Malaysia are free ($0 government fee) when submitted directly on the government portal within 3 days before arrival. Our handling time is the time our specialists take to inspect your passport scan, crop your photo to biometric standards, and submit your application to the government portal (within 6–12 hours for Standard handling, or within 2 hours for Priority handling). Government processing time is the time the destination country’s immigration department takes to review and approve the application after submission.

Unlike automated government portals that may reject an application days later due to photo dimensions or shadows, our specialists manually crop, center, and adjust backgrounds to meet official biometric standards—or email you immediately if a clearer scan is needed.

If you request a cancellation before our team submits your application to the official government portal, you receive a 100% refund of both the government fee and our service fee. Once filed with the government, statutory government fees are non-refundable, but if immigration declines your application, we refund 100% of the OnePassVisa service fee.
