Every price is calculated from one transparent formula: Statutory Government Fee + OnePassVisa Service Fee + any optional priority handling you choose = Total. There are no hidden fees or pre-selected add-ons. Official government fees were verified on 27 Oct 2026.

Our optional $29 service fee covers early intake at any time after booking your flight, specialist verification of passport and flight details, automated window scheduling, and PDF delivery.

Our handling time is how long our specialists take to verify your passport scan, crop your portrait photo, and submit your file to the government portal (6–12 hours for Standard handling, or within 2 hours if you choose Priority handling). Once submitted to the government, statutory government fees are non-refundable by law, but if your application is declined by immigration, we refund 100% of the OnePassVisa service fee.
