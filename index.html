Visa, ETA or arrival card? →
A visa, an electronic travel authorization (ETA / ESTA), and an arrival declaration are different entry requirements. Depending on your passport and destination, you may need one or both. Our destination guides and Travel Documents/ETAs section keep them clearly separated.
