Terms, Privacy, Refund Policy & Disclosures

Last Updated: 27 October 2026. Plain-language policies governing the use of OnePassVisa.com.

Terms of Service

Welcome to OnePassVisa.com, operated by OnePassVisa Digital Travel Services LLC ("OnePassVisa", "we", "our"). By submitting an application on our website, you engage OnePassVisa to review, format, and submit your electronic travel authorization, eVisa, or digital arrival card application on your behalf.

1. Scope of Private Assistance Service

OnePassVisa is a private, non-government filing service. When you use our service, you authorize our document specialists to review the personal and passport data you provide, format your uploaded images to meet official portal specifications, and submit your application to the relevant destination government authority.

2. Applicant Accuracy Obligation

You are responsible for ensuring that all passport numbers, dates of birth, expiration dates, and legal names match your physical passport booklet. If our specialists detect a discrepancy between your typed form and your uploaded passport bio-page scan, we will correct the typographical error or contact you for confirmation prior to submission.

3. Government Authority Discretion

Only the sovereign immigration authority of the destination country has the legal authority to grant, delay, or deny a visa, ETA, or entry permit, or to admit a traveler at a port of entry. Published government processing times are estimates based on official agency guidance.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

We maintain a clear, fair refund policy designed to protect travelers:

  • Cancellation Before Government Filing (100% Full Refund): If you contact support@onepassvisa.com to cancel your order before our specialists have submitted your application to the government portal, we will refund 100% of your payment (both the statutory government fee and the OnePassVisa service fee).
  • Application Declined by Immigration (100% Service Fee Refund): Once an application has been submitted to a government immigration portal, the statutory government fee is retained by the government and is non-refundable. However, if your application is declined by the government authority, OnePassVisa will refund 100% of our service fee (including any Priority handling fee).
  • Refund Processing Time: Approved refunds are credited back to your original payment method (Apple Pay, Google Pay, or payment card) within 3 to 5 business days.

Privacy Policy & Passport Data Security

OnePassVisa collects personal information—including full name, date of birth, passport number, contact details, passport bio-page scans, and portrait photographs—solely for the purpose of preparing and filing your requested travel document.

Data Encryption & Retention

  • All data transmissions are encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 (256-bit encryption).
  • Uploaded passport scans and portrait photographs are used exclusively for your visa or arrival card filing and are automatically purged after 30 days, or immediately upon your written request to support@onepassvisa.com.
  • We never sell, rent, or share personal data with third-party marketers or data brokers.

Non-Affiliation Disclaimer

OnePassVisa.com is an independent private agency and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by any government, embassy, consulate, or immigration ministry.

Travelers are never required to use a third-party service to obtain an eVisa, ETA, or digital arrival card. Every destination page on OnePassVisa.com provides a direct hyperlink to the official government portal where you can apply independently for the statutory government fee only (which is $0 for free official digital arrival cards such as the Thailand TDAC, Singapore SGAC, and Malaysia MDAC).

Cookie Policy & Consent Mode

We use essential browser storage (localStorage) to save your selected currency, your cookie consent preference, and any application draft you explicitly choose to save on your device.

Analytics and conversion measurement tags operate under Google Consent Mode v2 and default to denied until you click "Accept All" in the consent banner.
