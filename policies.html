Policies, Terms & Disclosures

Terms & Conditions of Service

Welcome to OnePassVisa.com (“OnePassVisa,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). These Terms & Conditions govern your use of https://onepassvisa.com and our private visa and travel document assistance services.

1.1 Scope of Services

OnePassVisa is a private, third-party application assistance agency. When you submit an order on our website, our services include:

  • Providing simplified online forms for Electronic Visas (eVisas), Electronic Travel Authorizations (ETAs/ESTAs), and Digital Arrival Cards;
  • Reviewing your passport bio-page scan and travel dates for completeness and accuracy;
  • Formatting digital photographs and PDF files to meet destination immigration standards;
  • Submitting your completed application to the relevant destination authority and emailing the issued travel document to you.

1.2 Government Authority Discretion

Only the sovereign government or immigration authority of your destination country can approve or deny a visa or travel authorization. OnePassVisa does not issue visas itself and cannot guarantee approval where an applicant is deemed ineligible by border authorities.

1.3 Fee Transparency

Every order clearly displays: (a) the statutory Government Fee, and (b) the OnePassVisa Service Fee for document review and processing. Travelers may alternatively apply directly on official government portals without paying our service fee.

GDPR & CCPA Compliant

Privacy Policy

Protecting your personal and passport information is our highest priority. This policy explains what data we collect and how we protect it.

2.1 Information We Collect

  • Passport Details: Full name, nationality, date of birth, passport number, issue/expiration dates, and a scan of your passport bio page.
  • Photographs: Digital facial photographs uploaded for visa formatting.
  • Contact & Travel Details: Email address, phone number, planned arrival dates, and accommodation address.

2.2 How We Use Your Data

We use your information strictly to review, format, and submit your requested visa or travel document application to the destination immigration authority and to send you order updates. We never sell or rent traveler data to third parties.

Clear Refund Terms

Refund & Cancellation Policy

3.1 Cancellation Before Government Submission (100% Refund)

If you request to cancel your order before your application has been submitted to the destination government portal, we will issue a 100% full refund of both the Government Fee and the OnePassVisa Service Fee.

3.2 Cancellation After Government Submission

Once an application has been submitted to the destination government’s immigration system, statutory government fees are non-refundable.

3.3 Optional Fee Refund Protection

Applicants who select optional Fee Refund Protection at checkout receive a 100% refund of both the Government Fee and OnePassVisa Service Fee if their application is denied by the immigration authority.
