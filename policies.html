Privacy Policy & Biometric Data Protection

At OnePassVisa.com, safeguarding your passport, biometric photographs, and personal travel records is our highest operational mandate. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, encrypt, process, transmit, and purge your personal data.

We process your personal and passport data exclusively for the purpose of auditing, formatting, and submitting your requested visa, ETA, or travel document application to the relevant sovereign government authority, and communicating real-time status updates to you.

OnePassVisa.com never sells, rents, trades, or monetizes traveler personal data or passport records. Your information is disclosed only to:

  • The official government immigration portal, embassy, or diplomatic mission of your chosen destination country;

Refund, Cancellation & Rejection Protection Policy

We stand behind our 68-point consular audit with a fair, transparent, and rapid Refund & Cancellation Policy.

3.1 Cancellation Prior to Government Submission (100% Full Refund)

If you wish to cancel your visa application before our specialist team has dispatched your dossier to the destination government portal—or if our pre-submission audit determines that your nationality is visa-exempt or ineligible—you are entitled to a 100% full refund of both the Government Immigration Fee and the OnePassVisa Service Fee.

3.2 Cancellation After Government Submission

Once your application has been formally lodged with the destination government’s immigration system, statutory government fees are immediately locked by the sovereign treasury and cannot be recalled. However, if we fail to meet the guaranteed audit turnaround window of your selected Express or Super Rush tier, we will refund 100% of your priority speed upgrade fee.

3.3 100% Visa Rejection Fee Refund Guarantee

Travelers who select the optional 100% Rejection Fee Refund Protection at checkout receive complete financial protection: in the rare event that your visa or ETA application is officially refused by the destination government (provided no fraudulent or forged documents were submitted), OnePassVisa will refund 100% of both the OnePassVisa Service Fee and the Statutory Government Fee within 24 business hours of receiving the refusal notice.

3.4 How to Request a Refund

Simply email billing@onepassvisa.com or submit a ticket on our 24/7 Support Desk quoting your OnePass Tracking ID (e.g., OPV-2026-XXXXX). Approved refunds are credited back to your original payment method within 2–5 business days.
