Terms & Conditions of Service

Welcome to OnePassVisa.com (“OnePassVisa,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). These Terms & Conditions of Service (“Terms”) constitute a legally binding agreement between you (“Applicant” or “User”) and OnePassVisa.com Inc. governing your use of https://onepassvisa.com and our online visa and travel document processing services.

1.1 Scope of Visa & Travel Document Services

OnePassVisa acts as a private visa and travel document processing service on behalf of the Applicant. Our services include:

  • Providing simplified online application forms for Electronic Visas (eVisas), Electronic Travel Authorizations (ETAs/ESTAs), and Digital Arrival Cards;
  • Performing a pre-submission specialist review of your passport bio-page scan, Machine Readable Zone (MRZ) lines, and travel dates;
  • Formatting and calibrating digital photographs and PDF attachments to conform to destination immigration requirements;
  • Submitting your completed application to the relevant government immigration authority and delivering the issued travel document via email and your online tracker.

1.2 Sovereign Authority Discretion

The destination government or immigration authority holds sole authority to approve, delay, or deny any visa or travel document application. OnePassVisa cannot guarantee approval where an applicant has prior immigration violations, security flags, or ineligible travel documents.

1.3 Accuracy of Applicant Information

Applicants are responsible for ensuring that all passport details, names, dates of birth, and declarations submitted are accurate and that their passport is valid for at least six (6) months beyond the planned arrival date unless otherwise specified.

1.4 Fee Breakdown

Each order clearly itemizes the statutory Government Fee and the OnePassVisa Processing Fee (plus any optional Express/Super Rush speed upgrades or Rejection Protection selected at checkout).

GDPR & CCPA Compliant

Privacy Policy & Data Protection

At OnePassVisa.com, protecting your passport and personal travel information is our highest priority. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, process, and protect your personal data.

2.1 Information We Collect

  • Passport & Identity Data: Full name, nationality, date of birth, passport number, issue and expiration dates, and passport bio-page scans.
  • Photographs: Digital passport-style photographs uploaded for visa formatting.
  • Travel & Contact Information: Arrival dates, accommodation details, email address, and phone number.

2.2 Purpose of Processing

We process your personal and passport data solely to verify, format, and submit your requested visa or travel document application to the destination immigration authority and to provide status updates.

2.3 Zero Third-Party Data Selling

OnePassVisa.com never sells, rents, or monetizes traveler data. Information is shared exclusively with the official destination immigration system required to issue your visa or travel document.

Clear Refund Terms

Refund, Cancellation & Rejection Protection Policy

3.1 Cancellation Before Government Submission (100% Refund)

If you request to cancel your application before our team has submitted your file to the destination government portal, you will receive a 100% full refund of both the Government Fee and the OnePassVisa Processing Fee.

3.2 Cancellation After Government Submission

Once an application is lodged with the destination government’s immigration system, statutory government fees are non-refundable. However, if we fail to meet the guaranteed review timeframe of an Express or Super Rush upgrade, we refund 100% of the upgrade fee.

3.3 100% Rejection Fee Refund Protection

Applicants who select optional Rejection Fee Refund Protection at checkout receive a 100% refund of both the Government Fee and OnePassVisa Processing Fee if their visa or travel document is officially denied by the destination immigration authority.

Data Subject Rights

GDPR, CCPA & Data Retention Schedule

OnePassVisa extends full EU GDPR and California CCPA privacy rights to all applicants worldwide:

  • Right of Access & Correction: Request a copy of your application records or correct passport details prior to filing.
